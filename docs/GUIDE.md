# Makkuro37: player's guide

Eight drones, one per column. Each column is one little soot creature on the screen. The knob above it
(K1–K8) and the two pads below it belong to that drone. Whatever sits in a column, the knob above it sets it.

**Lost?** Hold **KNOB BANK** for HELP. A tap on KNOB BANK always takes you back to the creatures.

## First sound in 30 seconds

1. At power-on you see **PRESETS**. Tap pads 1–14 to hear the presets. Use **< >** to switch theme
   (DRONES / ABRAXAS). Press the **green pad** (bottom right) to go to the creatures.
2. The awake creatures sing. Tap a **top pad** to wake or sleep its drone.
3. Turn **K1–K8**: each one tunes its drone, along the tuning set.
4. Push **fader 1** (CUTOFF) and **fader 2** (RESO), and move the **mod wheel**.
5. Play the **keys**: voices 4 + 5 are the keyboard VCOs.

## The voices

| Column | Sound |
|---|---|
| 1, 2, 6, 7 | **classic drone**: five oscillators in their own ranges, tuned to a chord; a subharmonic below |
| 3, 8 | **noise drone**: a tone bent or chopped by a slow square (sirens, birds), plus wind noise |
| 4, 5 | **VCO**: the keyboard voices (they sound only from the keys), with a sub-oscillator |

Every oscillator drifts a little, as analog ones do. WAVE sets saw, square, triangle or sine per voice. DETUNE
makes the generators beat slowly against each other.

## Pads

| Pad | |
|---|---|
| top 1–8 | the drones. **Tap**: wake, and it stays (HOLD). Tap again: sleep. **Hold**: it sounds only while held. Softer = slower swell. Pads 4 / 5 keep the last key's note sounding |
| bottom 1–5 | the 5-step sequencer's steps on / off (the white one is playing) |
| P (14) | PULSER: tap to start, tap in rhythm for the tempo, hold to stop |
| H (15) | HOLD ALL: everything that sounds stays on. Again: all off |
| C (16) | CHAOS: re-rolls all classic drones inside the scale. Hold C and tap a top pad: only that one |

In a menu, a setting with only two choices sits on its column's pads: the top pad is on (or choice A), the
bottom pad is choice B. A lit pad shows the current choice. In questions (and on PRESETS), **green** (pad 16,
bottom right) = OK and **red** (pad 15, next to it) = cancel.

## Keys and wheels

- **Two ways to play.** **SEQ PLAY** switches between them.
  - **SEPARATE** (default): the drones are tuned by their knobs and the keys play VCOs 4 + 5.
    KEYS menu (ARP): SINGLE / DUO / SPLIT, arpeggiator, latch, glide, vibrato, 16-step key sequence (STEP REC).
  - **FREE-PLAY**: each key wakes the next drone (1, 2, 3 …), tuned to that key: up to 8 at once.
    **SEQ REC** = HOLD: the notes keep sounding, and a held key pressed again lets it go.
- **OCTAVE − / +**: transpose the keys.
- **Pitch wheel**: bend (springs back). **Mod wheel**: closes the filter and raises its resonance.

## Faders

| | Bank A (filter) | Bank B (FADER BANK: effects) |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | CUTOFF | FX L blend |
| F2 | RESO | FX R blend |
| F3 | filter LFO rate | SPACE (reverb decay / delay time) |
| F4 | filter LFO depth | TAPE (wow, flutter) |

## Buttons: one menu each

Press the button again, or KNOB BANK, to return to the creatures. Use **< >** for the menu's pages.

| Button | Menu | Pages (K1–K8 = the 8 columns) |
|---|---|---|
| PARA | VOICE | WAVE of all 8; then one page per voice with its own eight values (tunes, VOLT, MUTE, MOD / the noise's tone and modulator / the VCO's wave, shape, octave, tune, FM, sync, EG) |
| CHORD | ENV | ATTACK, RELEASE, GATE (pad, sequencer, LFO, CASCADE: wakes when the voice before has swelled), VCO DECAY, SUSTAIN, EG |
| NOTE REPEAT | MOD | MOD SRC, MOD AMT, S&H, SUB, SUB DIV, DETUNE (the column's top pad switches its knob to the DETUNE rate) |
| GLOBE | MIX | VOLUME, PAN, TONE (warm ↔ bright), MASTER (master, analog drift, width, blends, tape, filter LFO) |
| SCALE | TUNING | tuning set (12-TET, just, Pythagorean, 24-TET, maqam Rast, slendro, pelog), root, SNAP (off = free cents, for beating), fine, glide; PITCH of all 8 |
| BT | LFO | LFO A / B rate and wave, joystick offsets, analog drift |
| SEQ | SEQ | 5 step levels, stages, pulser, run; MOTION: a loop of your pad taps and knob turns |
| FX | FX | FILTER (cutoff and resonance per side, LADDER / POLIVOKS / BAND / DIST, link, drive, CV); the two effect slots (PLATE, ROOM, SPRING, DELAY, SHIMMER, CHORUS, PHASER, CRUSH, RING, FREEZE, RESO) with X Y Z each; blends |
| ARP | KEYS | key mode, arpeggiator, rate, octaves, latch, glide, vibrato; STEP REC |
| PATCH | PRESETS | the presets; LOAD, SAVE (8 user slots: turn the slot's knob and confirm with green), MORPH (turn slot i's knob to glide into it). Hold PATCH: SETUP (pad light, USB console, OUTPUT LINE / SPEAKER) |

## Live tricks

- **HOLD ALL** (pad H) and **CHAOS** (pad C).
- **MORPH**: PRESETS, page MORPH. Turn a slot's knob and your sound glides into that scene.
- **Tap tempo**: tap pad P in rhythm.
- **Motion loop**: SEQ menu, MOTION page. Turn right to record your taps and turns, again to loop them, again to stop.
- **CASCADE**: set GATE to CASCADE on drones 2, 3 … Waking drone 1 then wakes the rest, one after another.
- **SPEAKER**: SETUP → OUTPUT SPEAKER for the built-in speaker. It cuts the lows and adds their harmonics.

## Update mode

Hold OCTAVE − and OCTAVE + for 5 seconds. Hold OCTAVE − while switching on for USB rescue.
